What do the repeated, long encoded-subdomain DNS queries to baddomain.net indicate?
Long, randomized/encoded subdomains queried repeatedly against one external domain is the classic signature of DNS tunneling — data is being smuggled out inside DNS queries, a channel that's often allowed through the firewall by default.
What best describes the SMB admin$ connection and remote process creation between WKS-114 and FIN-DB-02?
An admin-share connection immediately followed by a remotely spawned process, between two hosts that have never talked before, is the signature of lateral movement (PsExec-style remote execution) — the attacker pivoting from the first compromised host deeper into the network.
Which host is the original point of compromise, responsible for both the tunneling and the pivot?
WKS-114 is the first host seen tunneling data out, and it's also the source of the SMB connection into FIN-DB-02 — everything else follows from that one machine.
